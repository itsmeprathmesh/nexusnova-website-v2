# Phase 1 — Business Strategy

## Business Model
- **Type**: Project-based + retainer
- **Offer**: Custom AI automation systems for healthcare businesses
- **Revenue Streams**:
  - Discovery & Strategy Fee (one-time)
  - System Build (project-based, ₹50K–₹5L depending on scope)
  - Monthly Retainer (support, optimization, content updates)
  - Add-on Modules (appointment automation, patient recall, lead CRM)

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- **Primary**: Healthcare clinics in India (Nagpur, Pune, Mumbai, Bangalore)
  - Dental Clinics (single/multi-chair)
  - Skin & Cosmetic Clinics
  - Physiotherapy & Rehab Centers
  - Eye Clinics / Optical Chains
  - Multi-specialty Clinics
- **Size**: 3–20 staff, 50–500 patients/month
- **Pain Points**:
  - 20–40% patient no-show rate
  - Manual appointment booking via phone/WhatsApp
  - No systematic patient follow-up or recall
  - Scattered lead management
  - Generic website that doesn't convert

## Value Proposition
**Core**: "We automate your clinic operations so you can focus on patients."

**Not**: "We build websites with AI."

**Specific outcomes**:
- Reduce no-shows by 40%+ with automated reminders
- Capture every lead with CRM + instant follow-up
- Automate appointment booking 24/7
- Patient recall system for repeat visits

## Competitive Landscape
| Competitor Type | Weakness | Our Advantage |
|----------------|----------|---------------|
| Webflow/WordPress agencies | Template-based, no automation | Custom systems + automation |
| Healthcare SaaS (Zocdoc, Practo) | One-size-fits-all, costly | Tailored + affordable |
| Freelance devs | No process, no longevity | Structured delivery + support |
| DIY (Wix, etc.) | Generic, no conversion focus | Premium design + strategy |

## Differentiation
- **Full-stack**: Design + development + automation + ongoing support
- **Healthcare workflow expertise**: Not just a website — a patient operations system
- **Premium positioning**: Not a freelancer, not a template mill — a studio

## Customer Journey
1. **Awareness**: Google search ("dental clinic automation India"), LinkedIn, referral
2. **Consideration**: Reads case studies, checks solutions page, reviews process
3. **Decision**: Books strategy call → scoping → proposal → agreement
4. **Onboarding**: Discovery → Design → Build → Launch
5. **Retention**: Monthly support → reports → optimization → add-ons

## Revenue Targets
- Year 1: 12–15 projects at ₹50K–₹2L avg
- Year 2: 20+ projects + recurring retainer revenue
- Target MRR from retainers: ₹1L+ by month 18
